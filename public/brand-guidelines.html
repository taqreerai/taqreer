01 Brand

Arabic-first, intelligence-led.

taqreer.ai turns raw content into fully-designed presentations in Arabic and English — keeping the user's brand identity and giving them a real in-browser editor. Bidirectional (RTL/LTR) typesetting quality is the product's edge, so the brand reads as precise, calm, and quietly powerful.

Bilingual by design

Every surface mirrors with direction. Arabic and English are first-class — never an afterthought, never a bolt-on translation.

Flat & honest

Solid surfaces, hairline borders. No shadows, no glass, no fake depth. Clarity over decoration.

Intelligence has a color

Where the AI does work, the blue mesh appears. It's the one place the palette comes alive — used sparingly, it means something.

Voice: confident, plain-spoken, technically credible. We show the product working rather than describe it. Latin numerals (0–9) only — even inside Arabic copy.

02 Logo

The wordmark.

A single lowercase wordmark, taqreer, with the two e's drawn as stacked horizontal bars — a quiet nod to slides and lines of text. It is the only logo; there is no separate icon lockup beyond the bars.

Dark mode — white
Light mode — solid #0057FF

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on every side (the dashed frame). Never crowd it with type or graphics.

Minimum width: 96 px on screen / 22 mm in print, so the e-bars stay legible.

Do & Don't

✓ Do
Use solid blue on light surfaces and pure white on dark. Keep it flat and monochrome.
✕ Don't
Add a gradient, shadow, or outline; tint it any colour but the brand blue or white; or place white on a busy photo without a scrim.
✓ Do
Scale uniformly and preserve the clear space.
✕ Don't
Stretch, rotate, recolor to a non-brand hue, or separate the e-bars from the wordmark.

Assets in the app: /logo-blue.svg (solid blue, light mode) · /logo-light.svg (white, dark mode). The ThemeLogo component swaps them by theme automatically.

06 Glow & Gradients

Light, used with intent.

Two — and only two — places use color energy: the ambient aurora that grounds key pages, and the AI mesh that marks generative actions. Both are all-blue. Everywhere else stays flat.

Aurora — ambient page glow

Aurora. A soft blue wash anchored to the bottom of home, the creation flows and the editor. Heavy blur, drifting, masked so it never reaches center content. The same blue glow in both themes — only the base flips dark↔white beneath it.

AI mesh. Drifting blue blobs (#0042E0 → #1E6BFF → #3380FF → #66A3FF + a white spark). It appears as an animated border at rest and fills on hover. Only for generative surfaces — the Generate button, the active editor canvas. Never decorative.

Glow rule of thumb: if a surface isn't ambient atmosphere or an AI action, it gets no glow. Restraint is what makes the mesh read as "intelligence" rather than noise.

07 Principles

The seven rules.

1 · Flat & solid. No shadows, no blur, no transparency-as-depth. Surfaces are opaque fills with hairline borders.
2 · One accent: blue. #0057FF (light) / #3380FF (dark) — the one brand hue, one step brighter in dark.
3 · Cairo, both scripts. A single typeface for Arabic and Latin keeps bilingual layouts seamless.
4 · Direction-aware. Layouts mirror with locale. Use logical properties (inline-start/-end), never hard left/right.
5 · Latin numerals only. 0–9 even inside Arabic copy.
6 · Glow with intent. Aurora for ambience, mesh for AI. Nothing else glows.
7 · Dual-theme, equal care. Light and dark are both first-class. Verify every surface in both.
